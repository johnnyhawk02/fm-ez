Play with your keyboard (A W S E D F ...) or a MIDI keyboard.
Drag knobs up and down (Shift for fine), scroll to nudge, double-click to reset. The PC4 is this grid turned on its side: each PC4 row is one operator (Pitch, Envelope, Level), and its 4th knob is a Global. A ring dot means your PC4 knob is somewhere else: turn it past the value to pick it up. The earlier version with engines and randomising is at v1.
