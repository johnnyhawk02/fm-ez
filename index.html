Pick an engine, then play with your keyboard (A W S E D F ...) or a MIDI keyboard.
Drag knobs up and down (Shift for fine), scroll to nudge, double-click to reset, long-press or right-click to see and edit what a knob controls. Drop DX7 .syx banks anywhere on the page. A ring dot means your PC4 knob is somewhere else: turn it past the value to pick it up.
